# Podcast brief: About Me

## Questions

- Which earlier page is this not?
- Why is the alt longer than the person's name?
- What makes a nav link real?

## Explanations

The document, the outline, the portrait, the list, the aside, and the links are one grade split into four tests. `id` is separate from link text. Vague labels fail even on a link the brief did not list. The lab's About Me is a different checker.

## Example

A one-page bio for a classmate: one `h1`, three interests that are not Hiking, Cooking, and Music, and a footer link whose text is the site name. Ask what this exercise would reject about that page, and what it would still consider good structure.

## Misconceptions

- Passing Lab 01 finishes lesson 02.
- `Alex Rivera` is the right alt because it was right before.
- The word Interests in an `h2` creates `#interests`.
- A contact form belongs because the profile had one.

## Debugging scene

GitHub and Portfolio pass a manual look, and the test says it found `here`. Some other anchor uses that word. The scan is every `a`, not only the footer.
