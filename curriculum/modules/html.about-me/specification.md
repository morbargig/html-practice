## Objectives

1. Publish one English HTML5 document titled `About Me`, with a single `h1` inside `header` and no skipped heading levels.
2. In `main`, show `images/photo.jpg` with alt `Portrait of Alex Rivera`, an `h2` `Interests`, and the list `Hiking`, `Cooking`, `Music`.
3. Add nav `Interests` to `#interests` and `Profiles` to `#profiles`, an aside `Currently learning` / `Semantic HTML`, and `footer id="profiles"` with `GitHub` to `https://github.com/alexrivera` and `Portfolio` to `https://example.com`.
4. Keep every link's text visible, and reject `click here` and `here`.
5. Explain how this page differs from Alex Rivera's lesson 01 profile and from Lab 01's looser About Me check.

## Outcomes

A student can assemble the lesson 02 pieces into one page and can say which earlier page it must not be copied from.
