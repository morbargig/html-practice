There is no `check()` function and no Jest suite. The grade a teacher uses is the finished portal and the reflection, not a green Action.

## Objectives

1. In Exercise A, extend the starter with attributes and inputs the cell asks for, including the kinds of fields already sketched there.
2. In Exercise B, add another `fieldset` and `legend` named Course Preferences, add datalist suggestions, and explain how `datalist` differs from `select`.
3. In Exercise C, add FAQ `details` content, another popover, and a `dialog` opened and closed with `command` and `commandfor`.
4. In Exercise D, include a `progress` element and a `meter` element. The starter shows `progress` at 70 of 100 and a `meter` at 0.8. The task is to understand and use both, not to hit a secret number.
5. Build the final student portal from the TODO list in the last cell, in HTML only, and complete the self-check.
6. Preview dialogs and popovers in a current browser. Colab's preview can block them.

## Not a new practice folder

`select`, radio, checkbox, `fieldset`, `datalist`, `details`, popover, `dialog`, `progress`, and `meter` stay in this lab until a separate approval adds Jest exercises. Do not copy this notebook into `practices/` as part of the curriculum docs.

## Outcomes

A student can explain the lab's controls, finish the portal HTML, and say which behaviors must be checked outside Colab.
