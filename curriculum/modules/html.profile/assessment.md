# Assessment: the personal profile

## Traceability

| Objective | Exercise | Check | Slide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The three-item list | [01-unordered-list](../../../practices/lesson-01/05-personal-profile/01-unordered-list/README.md) | One `ul`. Items `Headings`, `Links`, `Images` | 1 |
| 2. Regions and the short bio | [02-page-regions](../../../practices/lesson-01/05-personal-profile/02-page-regions/README.md) | One of each landmark, nav hrefs, ids, `I am learning HTML.`, copyright | 2 |

## Criteria

- Item order matches.
- The two bios are not swapped.
- The student can click a fragment in the preview and explain a miss.
